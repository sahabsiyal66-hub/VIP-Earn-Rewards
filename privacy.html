Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Our Privacy Promise: VIP Earn & Rewards aims to collect only the minimum information necessary to operate its services and protect user accounts. We do not sell users' personal information.

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VIP Earn & Rewards handles information when visitors use our website and services.

2. Information We May Collect

When account registration becomes available, we may collect:

  • A unique VIP User ID.
  • A securely stored password hash, not the readable password.
  • Account creation and login records.
  • Coins, eligible activity and reward records.

We aim not to request unnecessary information such as CNIC, home address, contacts or phone number during ordinary registration.

3. Why We Use Information

Information may be used only where necessary to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Keep account balances and activity records accurate.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.
  • Provide support and operate the platform.
  • Meet applicable legal and payment requirements.

4. Password Security

Passwords must be protected using secure password hashing. We do not intend to store readable passwords. Users should never share their passwords or verification codes.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by necessary hosting, security, survey, reward or payment providers where required to operate a service, subject to applicable rules and disclosed purposes.

Information may also be disclosed when legally required by a valid request from an authorized authority.

6. Data Storage and Security

We plan to use appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect stored information. Access should be limited to authorized people who need it to operate and protect the platform.

No website or online storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, security, accounting or legal obligations. Deletion requests will be handled according to applicable law and any necessary record-retention requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

Some surveys, sponsored tasks or payment services may be provided by independent partners. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply when users choose to use those services.

9. Children's Privacy

The platform is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, security practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the official support contact published on our website.

Important: This policy describes our intended privacy approach. Actual data collection, storage, sharing and deletion practices must match the live system and applicable legal requirements before registration opens.
