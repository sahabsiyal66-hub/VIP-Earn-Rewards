
# VIP Earn & Rewards

Welcome to VIP Earn & Rewards!

Our goal is to build a trusted rewards platform where users can discover genuine sponsored tasks, surveys, and eligible reward opportunities.

## Our Mission

We aim to create a platform that benefits:
- Users who complete eligible activities.
- Advertisers who want genuine engagement.
- The platform through transparent and sustainable revenue.

## Planned Features

The following features are planned:

1. User registration and secure login.
2. User dashboard.
3. Rewards and coin balance.
4. Genuine sponsored tasks.
5. Approved survey partners.
6. Verified task completion.
7. Secure reward and withdrawal requests.
8. Admin dashboard.
9. Fraud prevention and account protection.
10. Payment options, subject to provider availability.

## Important Information

This project is currently under development.

User accounts, earning activities, coins, and withdrawals are not active yet.

No income or reward is guaranteed.

Users will never be asked to pay money just to join or unlock rewards.

## Payment Plans

Easypaisa is a preferred payment option for Pakistan, subject to confirmation of a genuine supported payment route.

International payment options will be considered after provider approval.

## Our Principles

- Honest earning opportunities.
- Transparent reward rules.
- User privacy and security.
- Genuine advertiser partnerships.
- Fair and verified payments.

## Technology

The website will be developed step by step with a secure backend and database.

Private API keys and partner secrets will never be stored in public frontend code.
